# Incident runbook

Triage every alert by confirming user impact, affected service, start time, and owner. If the
issue is false or already resolved, record evidence and close it. If active and reversible,
apply the smallest mitigation, verify recovery for 15 minutes, and record the change. If impact
was severe, data was at risk, or mitigation failed, preserve a timeline and open a postmortem
with impact, contributing factors, corrective actions, owners, and dates.
